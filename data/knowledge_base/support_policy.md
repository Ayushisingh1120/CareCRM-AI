# CareCRM Support Policy

## Password Reset

Customers who cannot access their account can request a password reset.

The password reset process sends a reset link to the customer's registered email address.

If the customer does not receive the reset email, support should verify the customer's email address and check whether the email was delivered successfully.

## Account Access

If a customer is unable to access their account after resetting the password, support should verify the account status.

If the account is locked, the issue should be escalated to the support team.

## Refund Policy

Refund requests should be reviewed against the customer's purchase and refund eligibility.

Support agents should not promise a refund before eligibility has been verified.

## Escalation

Issues involving account security, suspicious activity, or unresolved account access problems should be escalated to a human support agent.
